Masters: MLS/Zillow 1080p 30 fps 8–12 Mbps; vertical 1080×1920; portrait 1080×1350; square 1080×1080. No platform gave a real-estate loudness target — keep VO intelligible, no clipping.

## Fair Housing — words to avoid in VO/text/titles
"perfect for families", "child-free", "adults only", "young professionals", "empty nesters", "Christian community", "ethnic neighborhood", "safe", "exclusive", "private" (as neighborhood descriptors).
Use verifiable property facts: bedroom counts, "first-floor bedroom", "fenced yard", named amenities, accessibility facts stated neutrally. HUD digital-advertising guidance (29 Apr 2024) also covers targeting/delivery.

## Music and drones
Sync licence (composition) + master-use licence (recording). Zillow forbids copyrighted music. Drone: Part 107, registered aircraft, Remote ID, LAANC/authorisation, privacy/HOA, accurate labelling of off-property views.
