# Interview and documentary editing

## Workflow
1. **Organize** (Media page): bins for interviews, B-roll, archive, music, graphics, releases; metadata for subject,
   theme, location, take, function.
2. **Transcribe or outline** interviews; mark themes, contradictions, revelations, emotional turns with markers
   (`resolve-transcript-assembly` for structured SELECT/B-ROLL/FACT CHECK/LEGAL markers).
3. **String-outs and selects** timelines; keep full sources reachable — don't reduce to sound bites too early.
4. **Paper edit / story cards** → a structure: beginning (situation/question), middle (complication/change),
   end (consequence/landing). Large subject? find the character-driven through-line.
5. **Radio cut**: strongest interview/narration with minimal picture. *If it doesn't work with the screen off, B-roll
   and music won't save it.*
6. **Dialogue first**: sync, best performance + cleanest audio, remove repetition, keep breaths and pauses that carry
   meaning, J/L cuts for flow, room tone under edits.
7. **Then B-roll, archive, vérité, graphics, natural sound.**
8. Pace, ethics check, fact-check/clearances, mix, grade, caption, deliver.

## B-roll has a job
Illustrate, give evidence, reveal character, supply geography, cover a jump cut, create association, or give relief.
Not wallpaper. Keep the interview audio continuous on the main track, B-roll on an upper video track, **L-cut** so the
answer continues over the image, **J-cut** to introduce the next voice; keep the face for emotionally important
moments; use sequences (wide → medium → detail). Sometimes keeping the jump cut is more truthful than random cover.

## Ethical and structural checks (documentary)
Did rearranging statements change the meaning? Was necessary context removed? Does B-roll imply something the subject
didn't say? Are opposing perspectives fair? Does structure claim more than the evidence supports? Is music steering a
factual moment? Is there a clear question or through-line? Never combine quotes from separate contexts into a
misleading statement.

## In Resolve
Split edits: turn off **Linked Selection** (or trim audio and video independently), Place on Top (F12) for cutaways,
Sync Bin/multicam for second-camera cutaways (`resolve-guide-cut-edit`). Dialogue levels/repair in Fairlight
(`resolve-guide-fairlight`). Judgment calls — meaning, attribution, fairness, legal — stay with a human.
