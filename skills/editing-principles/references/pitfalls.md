# Editorial pitfalls to scan for (use as a review checklist)

- Cutting to the speaker every time; no listener reactions.
- Jump cuts that are accidental (same size/angle) rather than motivated.
- Cuts at dialogue endpoints only; never J/L cuts, so the edit feels mechanical.
- Continuity: crossed axis without a bridge, flipped eyeline, prop/hand mismatch, lighting direction change.
- Transition stacking; dissolve used where a cut would do; wipes with no reason.
- Every pause removed (silence can be the beat); every shot the same length.
- Music edited to the beat but against the emotional beat; music announcing every emotion.
- Test-audience note taken literally: audiences find *that* something fails, not *why* — look for missing setup
  earlier before deleting the flagged scene (Murch).
- Fix-in-colour/sound for a problem that is editorial.
