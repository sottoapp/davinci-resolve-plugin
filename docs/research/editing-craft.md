# Editing craft — digest

One `deep` run (2026-11-22) plus the Murch video analysis. Feeds `editing-*` skills. Craft claims are widely taught
conventions, not measurements; Murch's weights are his own heuristic.

Key points: Rule of Six order and weights (emotion 51 / story 23 / rhythm 10 / eye-trace 7 / screen plane 5 / 3D
continuity 4); cuts as blinks/thought punctuation; cut vocabulary (match-on-action, J/L, cutaway, smash); 180° rule and
coverage; radio cut and paper edit for documentary; B-roll must have a job; sound-first dialogue editing; vertical
short-form is a rebuild not a crop; review in distinct passes and classify notes.
