|---|---|---|---|---|
| event-editing-program-iso | event-video-editing | 3/5 | 5/5 | the control built a sensible hybrid but did not say to check sync at start, middle and end, and did not say to open the recap on the strongest moment rather than a logo; with-skill also set a one-line brief per deliverable and named angles by function. |

| event-compliance-sponsor-slides | event-delivery-compliance | 5/5 | 5/5 | the control covered sync vs performance licence, slides, sponsors, attendees and "not legal advice"; with-skill added the go/no-go list, minors, captions, union/venue checks and dated platform specs. |

Reading: the skills are most useful where Resolve edition and verification details matter (Studio flags, sync checkpoints); on general rights questions the baseline was already sound. No skill text was changed. These tests do not check the claims against real event footage.
