# Event graphics, titles and transitions — research digest

Date: 2026-11-22. One `deep` run (27 sources: motion-graphics and social-format guides, Blackmagic training, Fusion macro documentation) plus videos. Brand and platform guidance is secondary; safe-zone figures change by platform.

## Visual system first
Primary styles: hard cuts, restrained zooms, directional whips, selective speed ramps, light hits, kinetic type. Structural graphics: opener, countdown, session cards, lower thirds, sponsor bumpers, slide layouts, captions, end card. Brand controls: approved fonts and weights, colour references, logo versions, clear space, minimum size, background restrictions, approved animation behaviour. Separate 16:9, 1:1/4:5 and 9:16 layouts, not shrunken landscape. No more than two type families; a backing shape or shadow over busy footage.

## Recap and sizzle language
Hard cuts by default. Speed ramps on entrances, crowd, sport, performance, hands, lights, demos, anchored to a beat; Retime Controls speed points and a smoothed Retime Curve; Speed Warp (Studio) only where it matters. Whip: match direction, speed, blur, exposure and the point of maximum smear; ideally shot both ends; a whip against the movement reads as an error. Zoom and crash zoom for beat drops, wide-to-speaker, new section: keyframed scale/position, blur, short sound accent, centred on the subject, within resolution. Glitch for technology branding, chapter break, sponsor ident, brief and clear of names, captions and legal text. Light leaks, flares and flashes for concerts, stage-light transitions, logo stings, beat hits; not to hide a bad cut.
Video findings: Push transition as a fast, repeatable replacement for manual title or graphic keyframes (about 12 frames, Start on edit, ease In and Out, copy the transition between cuts); Edge Wipe for reveals and grade comparisons; an Adjustment Clip with a keyframed zoom (1.0 to 1.2) for a uniform slow push; Drop Shadow as a Resolve FX so settings paste across clips; Blanking Fill (Fill Mode Zoom, source crop 0, fade colour not white) for vertical in horizontal; the "Resolve 21 speed ramp transition" tutorial is a keyframed Retime Frame ramp with Ease Out and Ease In across a cut and requires handles on both clips.

## Core package
- Opener 5–12 s: venue, fast details (badges, hands, lights, signage), event name and edition, logo, first beat or speaker line; no long logo animation first.
- Countdown: dominant number, consistent rhythm, clear final transition; 5, 10, 30 minute variants.
- Session and chapter cards: same in/out timing throughout, simple.
- Lower thirds: full name over role or company; two versions (first ID with title and company, repeat ID short); inside title safe, away from faces, microphones, slides and captions; short entrance and exit; redesign for vertical.
- Sponsor: sting 1–2 s, bumper 3–5 s, full slate longer; supplied artwork, clear space, no stretching, recolouring or effects unless the brand guide allows; editable controls for logo, accent, background, timing.
- Slide layouts: speaker large and slide small when delivery matters, slide large when content matters, consistent corner, border, shadow, crop; never an unreadable thumbnail; a Fusion composition with exposed controls.
- Captions: dedicated subtitle track when they must be exported or switched; one or two lines, natural breaks, correct names and terms, above mobile UI, not colliding with lower thirds; check the rendered file on a phone.
- End card: logo, one action, URL/handle/date, optional sponsor line; variants for recap, registration, replay, social.

## Reusable titles
Build in Fusion, select nodes, Macro > Create Macro, expose only what editors need (name, role/company, headline, approved style, brand colours, logo on/off and version, position, animation duration, entry/exit style, background opacity, safe-area position). Modular: text, shapes, logo input, published animation controls, MediaOut, notes. Separate macros per aspect ratio (`EVENT_LowerThird_16x9_v01`, `_9x16_`, session card, sponsor bumper, PIP, end card). Power Bin tree: brand guides, logos, font references, music/SFX, Fusion titles, transitions, sponsor assets, safe-area overlays, export presets; version a master library and do not overwrite a template another project is using. Video findings: a finished title clip dragged into a Power Bin carries all Inspector settings and keyframes; Power Bins are shared across projects in the same Project Library; right-click Export Bin makes a `.drb` that does not copy media; the Fusion "Keyframe Stretcher" keeps intro and outro when the clip length changes; a built-in Fusion title had an outro that never animated off and needed a manual fix; Text+ keyframe timing is edited in Fusion, not the Edit page; third-party MotionVFX packs are paid and can stutter.

## Safe areas, brand QA, performance, Studio
Action about 93%, Title about 90%, Center; mobile: real 9:16 timeline, platform overlay, preview at phone scale, test the busiest background behind captions, bottom and right UI zones, unlisted test upload. Brand QA: event name and year, speaker spelling and title, approved logo and orientation, clear space, colours, no unauthorised gradients or shadows, consistent timing, no clipped text, no collision with captions or platform UI, sponsor order and prominence per contract, fonts installed or packaged.
Performance: proxies or optimised media, fast cache drive, Render Cache Smart, Render Cache Fusion Output or Render in Place on finished titles, pre-render heavy openers and sponsor animations, resize large stills, test a short range, separate timelines for master, social, captioned, sponsor; Fusion proxy mode does not reduce comp resolution reliably. Studio-dependent: Neural Engine tools, temporal and spatial NR, motion blur, Speed Warp, Magic Mask, transcription, AI voice and dialogue tools, additional Resolve FX; most graphics from Edit-page titles, Text+, masks, transforms, merges and ordinary Fusion nodes do not need Studio; a third-party template depends on the nodes it uses.

## Mistakes
Fancy transition on every cut; whip without matching direction and blur; ramps without frame-rate headroom; glitch, flares or zooms obscuring a speaker or sponsor; captions too small; graphics where platform UI covers them; mixed fonts, easing and colours; every title built from scratch; too many exposed template controls; unapproved logo files; lower thirds under captions; rendering the whole timeline before testing the problem section; trusting a smooth cached preview; no phone, sound-off check.
