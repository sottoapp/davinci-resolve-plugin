# Event structures, B-roll and same-day plan — reference

Source: the Sotto DaVinci Resolve knowledge base (Notion) › Vertical: Event (secondary, practitioner guides). Times are conventions.

## Recap (about 2–4 min, adapt for 60–90 s)
- 0:00–0:05 strongest moment (applause, reveal, quote, play, reaction).
- 0:05–0:20 scale: exterior, registration, signage, room-wide, crowd, sponsor branding.
- 0:20–1:30 message and experience: keynote or panel soundbites, speaker close-ups, audience reactions, networking, product demos, booths, volunteers/performers/athletes.
- Close: applause, awards, testimonials, group moments, final line, event identity, CTA or "see you next year".
- Montage shots 1–3 s; hold important emotional or informational shots; cut on movement, gesture, applause or phrase; alternate wide, medium, close, detail, reaction; build to a peak instead of holding maximum speed.

## Sizzle (30–60 s)
Non-chronological, music-led, one or two soundbites, brand marks motivated by the story, a registration or sponsor CTA at the end.

## Sports, festival, gala, fundraiser, concert
Add preparation, anticipation, hands, costumes, signage, audience emotion, volunteers, winners, donors, performers and aftermath. Concerts: keep the desk mix for clarity and room mics for scale.

## B-roll list to brief the shooters
Exterior and set-up · signage, badges, stage, sponsor details · arrival and registration · wide crowd and room density · speaker and stage · reactions, note-taking, laughter, applause · networking · booths and demos · interviews and testimonials · closing, awards, group shots · teardown. Wide, medium and close of each idea; hold 10–15 s; frame center-safe for 9:16.

## Full session timeline checklist
Trim pre-roll and false starts; cleanest angle default; close-ups for emphasis, speaker change and reactions; slides or screen capture whenever they carry information; opening slate, session title, speaker strap, closing slate; audience questions preserved; chapter markers; one full watch of the export for sync, slide timing, audio continuity and graphics.

## Speaker clip
First sentence hooks; remove filler and repetition; cover jump cuts with B-roll, slides or an alternate angle; name and title strap; burned-in captions for social; end on a complete thought or CTA. A speaker reel uses the best 1–3 minutes, not a summary of every topic.

## Same-day or next-day plan
Before: project and bins, title templates, logos, music chosen and licensed, 16:9 and 9:16 timeline templates, export presets, transfer method and edit station tested, marker vocabulary, board feed, slide capture and backup paths tested.
During: operators hand off cards by session; an assistant verifies copies; the editor syncs and bins continuously; a producer marks key moments and confirms speaker names; selects are built while later sessions record.
Priorities: one music track, one clear story, a few strong quotes, reliable sound, simple branded titles, social-safe shots, no experimental effects. Export a review version early and reserve time to play it on the venue's real screen. A 48-hour turnaround becomes practical only when ingest and review run through the event.

## Templates
Timelines: recap 16:9 and 9:16, sizzle 60 s, speaker clip 90 s, sponsor reel 2 min, full session, social 30 s captioned, each with frame rate, tracks, dialogue/music/ambience/SFX buses, loudness meter, caption style, safe-title guides, adjustment clips and intro/end placeholders.
Deliver presets: master archive (ProRes or DNxHR), review H.264/H.265 with watermark or timecode, 16:9 web, 9:16 social, captioned social, audio-only, per-session. Predictable names with version and aspect, for example `EVENT_DAY01_KEYNOTE_FULL_v03_16x9.mp4`.
