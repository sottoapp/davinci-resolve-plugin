# Short-form vertical editing

A short-form version is **not a cropped long-form edit**: rebuild the opening, information order, shot duration, text
placement and ending for a small vertical screen.

## Resolve workflow
1. **Duplicate** the horizontal timeline — never damage the master.
2. Set the duplicate's **timeline resolution to 9:16** (commonly 1080×1920) and scaling to crop/fill (no pillarbox).
3. **Reframe** each shot (Inspector ▸ Video ▸ Transform: Zoom/Position); use **Smart Reframe** (Studio) as a first
   pass, then inspect and correct its keyframes.
4. Protect faces, hands, product and on-screen information; keep titles/captions inside platform-safe areas
   (UI overlays crowd the bottom and edges).
5. **Hook first** — move the strongest line/visual to the start; drop introductions that give no immediate value.
6. Tighten: shorten pauses and repeats (`resolve-tighten-recording`; Clip ▸ Audio Operations ▸ Ripple Delete Silence
   is one route), vary visual information often enough to hold attention but let key moments land.
7. Captions, emphasis words, sound effects and music are part of the rhythm, not decoration (`resolve-subtitles`).
8. Export (`resolve-delivery-specs` for platform baselines) and **watch the file on a phone**.

## Craft checks
First 2 seconds earn the next 5 · one idea per clip · cut on motion and on emphasis words · punch-ins for jump-cut
variety (Zoom + Position Y to eye level) · audio clean and loud enough on phone speakers · loop or land the ending
deliberately.
