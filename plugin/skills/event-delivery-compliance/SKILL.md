# Event delivery and compliance

Event video publishes other people's faces, brands, slides and music. Guidance from location, sponsorship, GDPR/ICO, WCAG, PRO-licensing and platform sources (the DaVinci Resolve Context page (Notion) › Vertical: Event), dated 2026-11-22.
**Not legal advice**: law, collective agreements, venue contracts, sponsor agreements and platform rules control and vary by jurisdiction. Say so, and verify specs on the upload screen before delivery.

## Clearance register (before filming; check again before publishing)
| Item | Confirm |
|---|---|
| People | speaker or performer release; attendee notice or consent basis; minor and guardian consent |
| Location | who grants rights, permit AND property agreement (they are different), rooms, hours, crew, insurance |
| Brands | sponsor agreement, approved logo files, placement, approvals |
| Music | live, background, sync, master, mechanical, platform rights as applicable |
| Slides | speaker licence plus third-party image, video, chart, font and music permissions |
| Deliverable | owner, permitted edits, territory, term, media, monetisation, captions, takedown |

## Rules
1. **Sponsors**: only the unmodified approved logo, placed as contracted (screen, lower third, holding slide, end card, social cutdowns, minimum time, equal treatment). Missing contracted exposure is a delivery defect, so check it in QC. If a logo cannot be cleared use a text acknowledgement. Paid partnerships carry the platform's disclosure.
2. **Speakers and performers**: signed release before recording that covers recording, slides, editing and clips, subtitles and translation, channels, term, territory, bio and quote use, takedown. Recording is not publishing. Union talent: identify the agreement first.
3. **Attendees**: layered notice (registration terms, entrance and room signs, announcement), a real opt-out route (no-camera zone, lanyard, seating), crew opt-out list, blur or cut those who opted out. A notice is transparency, not necessarily consent; close-ups, portraits and advertising may need consent, and GDPR basis must be assessed per use for EU/UK-linked events.
4. **Minors**: assent plus written guardian consent (mechanism varies); no names, school or location metadata; no close-ups without consent; do not livestream children unless assessed; flag safeguarding or court-order concerns.
5. **Venue and labour**: confirm authority, areas, camera positions, rigging, drones, signage, AV exclusivity, house feeds, recording fees, final-edit approval and union jurisdiction in writing. A booked room is not permission for lobbies and corridors.
6. **Music**: venue and DJ performance licences do not clear sync. Video needs sync (composition) plus master (recording); live performance recorded for publication needs more. Library licences list platforms, paid media, territory and sublicensing to clients and sponsors. No "30-second rule". Keep invoices and licences; mute or replace if unclear.
7. **Slides and third-party content**: collect decks early, speaker warrants rights; internet availability, attribution or brevity does not authorise recording or publishing; record the cleared deck, crop or replace uncleared slides, get separate permission for PDFs, transcripts and social clips; do not assume fair use.
8. **Captions**: target WCAG 2.2 AA unless law or contract differs (captions for prerecorded 1.2.2, live 1.2.4, audio description 1.2.5, speaker ID and meaningful sound). Auto-captions are a draft (`resolve-subtitles`). US rules differ by entity (ADA Titles II and III, Section 508).
9. **Specs** (`resolve-delivery-specs`): publish a master, platform encodes, caption file, transcript, stills, stems if contracted, cue sheet, rights and consent report. Reported numbers on 2026-11-22: YouTube 8 Mbps 1080p SDR and 35–45 Mbps 4K at 24–30 fps; LinkedIn MP4/MOV up to 5 GB and about 10 minutes for Pages; Instagram a separate 9:16 cut. They change, verify them.
10. **Retention and privacy**: master archive, access copy, raw footage only while needed, consent and rights register; encrypted storage, role-based access, MFA, vendor agreements, deletion that reaches caches, exports and backups; no facial recognition without a separate assessment; livestreaming is processing.

## Go/no-go gate (do not publish until you can show each)
Venue permission and permit record · sponsor approvals · speaker, performer and minor releases · attendee notice and opt-out log · music, slide and third-party clearances · union and crew confirmation · caption QA record · platform export and accessibility check · named owner for retention, takedown and incidents.

## Mistakes
Treating a venue's music licence as sync · "recording permission" taken as publishing permission · notice assumed to be consent · uncleared slides published · logos stretched or recoloured · missed contracted sponsor exposure · broadcast loudness at an online target · unproofed auto-captions · raw footage kept indefinitely.
